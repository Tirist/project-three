# Product Vision

## Product Concept
OperatorSim is a mobile-web, voice-first training platform that compresses an operational workday into a focused 5–10 minute simulation. Learners interact with realistic events as they unfold over simulated time, making decisions under pressure.

The product is designed to feel systemic and emergent: each decision changes the operating context, customer state, and downstream events. Visual presentation stays simple so cognitive load is focused on operations and communication.

## Target Users
Primary users for MVP:
- Frontline employees at small and medium businesses (starting with brewery/taproom operations).
- Shift leads or trainers who need repeatable, short training loops.

Secondary users:
- Managers configuring company tone, escalation rules, and training priorities.

## MVP Scope (Product Level)
MVP focuses on one vertical (brewery/taproom) and one training loop:
1. Start a simulated shift.
2. Progress through event-driven interactions over a compressed game day.
3. Respond via voice-first flow with tap/click fallback.
4. Receive end-of-session evaluation and coaching summary.

## Why This Is Not an LMS or Quiz Game
OperatorSim differs from standard LMS modules and quiz systems in key ways:
- **Time-based operations model**: events arrive as part of a day flow, not isolated question cards.
- **Stateful customers**: customers have memory, mood, patience, and communication style that influence outcomes.
- **Operational trade-offs**: player choices affect queue pressure, service quality, and escalation risk.
- **Deterministic engine + AI layer split**: simulation truth comes from rules, while AI provides human-like dialogue and coaching.
- **Replay variability**: procedural event and customer combinations produce different runs with the same core goals.

## Product Value
- Gives teams frequent practice in short intervals.
- Builds judgment under realistic operational constraints.
- Creates consistent coaching signals without requiring constant live manager oversight.
